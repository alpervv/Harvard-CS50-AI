# Analysis

## Layer 8, Head 5

This head is paying attention to adjectives.

Example Sentences:
- A giant cat ate his [MASK].
- He saw a big dog in the [MASK].
- He saw a big [MASK] bear.

## Layer 8, Head 10

I have observed that this head pays attention to direct object. In the presence of the determiner "the" inside the direct object, more attention is paid to "the".

Example Sentences:
- He has [MASK] liked me.
- [MASK] sees the dog.
- They enjoyed the [MASK] last night.
- He missed the bus and kicked [MASK].

## Layer 7, Head 10

This head, like layer 8 head 10, pays attention to direct objects as well.

Example Sentences:
- He has [MASK] liked me.
- [MASK] sees the dog.
- They enjoyed the [MASK] last night.
- He missed the bus and kicked [MASK].
